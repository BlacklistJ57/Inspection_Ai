👋 How it works

  1. Register a device — fill the form and tap Register & Generate QR.
  2. The app creates a signed QR code. Download it as PNG or print it.
  3. Stick the QR on the device (or keep it with the record).
  4. Later, open the Scan tab and point the camera at the QR.
  5. ✅ Valid QR → ACCESS GRANTED with full device info.
    ❌ Invalid → denied.

Everything runs offline. No data leaves this device.
